# MusicMania on iPhone

On iPhone, MusicMania runs as a web app: no App Store needed. Your stats are computed and stored
**on your iPhone**.

**Link:** https://tb18003.github.io/musicmania/

## 1. Install it (1 minute)

1. Open the link in **Safari** (other browsers cannot install it).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen** and tap **Add**.
4. Open MusicMania from the new icon on your home screen. From now on, always use that icon, not
   Safari.

## 2. Sign in with Spotify

1. Tap **Sign in with Spotify** and accept the permissions on Spotify's page.
2. If Spotify says your account is not allowed, ask whoever sent you the link to add you: the app
   accepts only 5 accounts. They will need your Spotify account's name and e-mail.

## 3. Open it often

Spotify only shares your **last 50 songs**. MusicMania saves them each time you open it, so open
it at least once a day, or twice if you listen a lot. If too much time passes, the app warns you
that some songs may be missing.

## 4. Import your whole history (optional, recommended)

This shows your all-time stats, not just from today:

1. At [spotify.com/account/privacy](https://www.spotify.com/account/privacy/), tick **Extended
   streaming history** and request your data.
2. Spotify e-mails you a link, usually within a few days (it can take up to 30). Download the ZIP
   to the **Files** app.
3. In MusicMania: **Settings → Import Spotify history** and choose the ZIP.

The file is processed on your iPhone and never uploaded. IP addresses and locations in the export
are ignored.

## 5. Join the ranking (optional)

In the **Ranking** tab, tap **Share my stats**. Only totals are shared (minutes, streams, streaks,
unique artists and your top 10 per period), **never your history**. You can stop sharing and
delete your server data at any time in **Settings → Ranking**.

If you see "You are not on the list yet", tap **Copy Spotify ID** and send that ID to whoever runs
the ranking.

## Quick answers

- **Can my data disappear?** Not while you use the app from the home screen. Just in case,
  **Settings → Export history** saves a JSON copy.
- **Does it work offline?** Yes: everything already saved shows offline. New songs are saved when
  you are back online.
- **How do I remove it?** **Settings → Sign out** deletes all data from the iPhone. Then press and
  hold the icon and remove it.
